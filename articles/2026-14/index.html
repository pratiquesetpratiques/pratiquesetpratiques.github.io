14. Marie Trezanini, "Setting Ancient Egyptian Poetry to Music, Then Getting Montpellier Middle Schoolers to Sing in Ancient Egyptian," Bridging Ancient Worlds, 8 p. 10.5281/zenodo.23137840.

Abstract
The article presents NASHAQ and a Montpellier workshop where students explore Ancient Egyptian poetry, Eastern Mediterranean rhythms, writing systems, and multilingual song composition, linking artistic creation, cultural mediation, and our heritage.
